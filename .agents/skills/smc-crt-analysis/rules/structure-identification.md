## Market Structure Identification

Identify structure on the highest relevant timeframe first, then drill down.

**Incorrect (no structural context):**

Jumping to LTF entries without identifying HTF trend direction and key structural levels.

```
- Looking at M5 chart directly
- Taking trades without knowing if HTF is bullish or bearish
- Ignoring BOS/CDC confirmations
```

**Correct (top-down structural analysis):**

```
1. Identify structure on HTF:
   - Bullish: HH + HL. BOS confirms when price breaks previous HH.
   - Bearish: LH + LL. BOS confirms when price breaks previous LL.
   - Consolidation: EQH + EQL — range-bound.
   - CDC/CHoCH: First break of opposing swing = potential reversal.

2. Classify strong vs weak points:
   - Strong Low = created by a BOS upward (protected, not expected to break)
   - Weak High = not yet broken upward (liquidity target)

3. Use three types of structural breaks:
   a. Wick-to-wick mapping, wick break
   b. Wick-to-wick mapping, body break (used for CHoCH)
   c. Body-to-body mapping, body break (most conservative)
```
