## Risk Management

Strict risk rules to preserve capital and maintain edge over time.

**Incorrect (poor risk management):**

```
- No predefined stop loss
- Risking more than planned per trade
- Taking trades with R:R below minimum
- Continuing to trade after multiple losses
- Thinking in dollar amounts instead of percentages
```

**Correct (disciplined risk management):**

```
Position sizing formula:
P = (K × R) / (Entry - StopLoss)
where K = account capital, R = risk percentage

Rules:
- Max 2 stop losses per day → stop trading
- Min R:R 1:5 for sniper entries, 1:3 for swing
- SL below second swept low (not first)
- Always calculate and accept risk BEFORE entering
- Think in percentages, not dollars
```
